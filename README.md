# SemiResearch AI — VAC Capstone 12

## AI Research Report on the Semiconductor Industry

**Research Topic:** India's Semiconductor Mission and the Growth of India's Chip Industry

**Research Cut-off:** 18 October 2026

### Project Objective

To demonstrate how multiple AI tools can be combined to produce an evidence-based research report through independent research, AI-output comparison, source verification, hallucination analysis, content creation, visual presentation, and evidence tracking.

### Research Questions

1. What are the main objectives, policies, and incentives of India's Semiconductor Mission?
2. What semiconductor manufacturing, assembly, testing, packaging, and chip-design projects have been announced or developed in India?
3. What opportunities, challenges, and risks affect India's ambition to become a semiconductor hub?

### Four AI Tools Used

| AI Tool | Role in the Project |
|---|---|
| **Perplexity** | Independent semiconductor research and source discovery |
| **Gemini** | Second independent research output and comparison |
| **NotebookLM** | Source-grounded analysis using selected official government materials |
| **ChatGPT** | Prompt engineering, comparison organization, synthesis, report drafting, and presentation content |

AI-generated responses were treated as research inputs, not as final factual authorities.

### Research Methodology

1. Define three semiconductor research questions.
2. Design structured prompts.
3. Conduct independent research using Perplexity and Gemini.
4. Use NotebookLM for analysis grounded in selected official sources.
5. Compare AI-generated claims and identify differences.
6. Verify important claims against government and official-source material.
7. Classify outdated, conflicting, incorrect, and unsupported claims.
8. Prepare the final research report, charts, and presentation.
9. Maintain a source log for evidence tracking and review.

**Workflow:**

Structured Prompts → Independent AI Research → NotebookLM Grounding → Cross-Tool Comparison → Official-Source Verification → Error Analysis → Human Review → Final Deliverables

### Key Research Findings

- Semicon India Programme 1.0: ₹76,000 crore outlay.
- Semicon 2.0: ₹1,27,500 crore outlay, approved on 15 July 2026.
- Twelve approved semiconductor manufacturing proposals, with reported investment exceeding ₹1.64 lakh crore.
- Portfolio includes one silicon fab, two compound/specialty semiconductor fabs, and nine packaging-related projects.
- Official updates used in the study report five identified commercial/operational semiconductor units by September 2026.
- India's semiconductor ambitions depend on investment, manufacturing execution, design capability, infrastructure, supply-chain development, and specialist talent.

These findings reflect the study's stated research cut-off and should not be interpreted as continuously updated project statuses.

### Responsible AI and Limitations

- AI outputs were not treated as primary factual sources.
- Research claims were compared with selected official materials.
- Outdated and unsupported claims were documented where identified.
- Project statuses and investment figures may change after the research cut-off.
- Some individual claims require additional source-level verification.
- The original AI responses and a completed live automation demonstration are not included in the repository.
- Copyright, citation practices, and human review are discussed in the research report.

### Conclusion

SemiResearch AI demonstrates how Perplexity, Gemini, NotebookLM, and ChatGPT can support different stages of an evidence-based research workflow. The project examines India's growing semiconductor ecosystem while emphasizing source verification, transparent comparison, responsible AI use, and human review.
